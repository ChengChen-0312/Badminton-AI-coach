# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to:
- be respectful and constructive
- focus on technical content and evidence
- welcome feedback and different viewpoints

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- harassment, abuse, or personal attacks
- discriminatory language
- deliberate disruption of discussion or review

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy.
Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent participation restrictions.
